About Clinical Appointment System

Our Mission

To provide a user-friendly online platform that makes it easier for patients to book and make appointments, also to keep medical reports safe and easy to retrieve.

Our Vision

To become the leading healthcare information platform that improves and easen the process of booking and sheduling appointments making it convinient to both patients and healthcare workers.

Steps on how to use the Clinical Appointment System

  1. Log in with your authorized healthcare provider credentials
  2. Search or register a patient to open their digital chart
  3. Review their medical history ,laboratory results and medications
  4. Update the patients record
  5. Logout after every session to secure the patients records
